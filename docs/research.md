# Selection and source notes

Checked 17 November 2026. TUNE adds the affiliate-conversion-review family, absent from the registry before this run. It concerns direct partner conversion decisions, not partner recruitment, influencer campaign delivery, sales employee commissions or dropship orders.

[TUNE Scale pricing](https://www.tune.com/solutions/advertisers/compare-plans/) lists $1,500 per month paid annually, equivalent to $18,000 annually. The page does not name the dollar currency. Onboarding and partner payments are fee based, and marketplace network and processing fees are additional. No verified NZ or AU invoice was found. Do not present this calculation as a customer bill or a saving.

Buyer fit is inferred for agencies and direct brands; local customer and CRM repository searches found no confirmed TUNE subscription. Live CRM was not read. Search competition is visible in [G2's TUNE alternatives page](https://www.g2.com/products/tune/competitors/alternatives), but no search-volume measurement was obtained.

Its [export guide](https://helpdesk.everflow.io/customer/how-to-export-your-data) establishes an export path, but the base does not import Everflow.
