# Own the review record

An affiliate programme has recurring decisions about conversions, returns, partner terms and the amounts being reviewed. Those facts belong to the business. They can live in a database with commands that preserve the source, the decision and the evidence behind it.

This base replaces the manual review desk around exported conversion records. Live tracking and payment infrastructure are separate work. The useful starting point is a reliable account of what was imported, what changed, who reviewed it and what still needs attention.

Omni by Enterprise DNA builds and runs a version for the business, with its fields, rules, data migration and required connections. One setup fee, then a retainer. The free base stays open source.
