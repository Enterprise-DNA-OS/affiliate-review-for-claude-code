# Record checks and their sources

Checked 17 November 2026. The command checks stored evidence and dates. It does not inspect a live advertisement, give legal approval, validate a payment or establish compliance for the business.

| Check | What the command flags | Source and boundary |
|---|---|---|
| DISCLOSURE-EVIDENCE | An active partner has no inspection, or its latest inspection per placement is missing, unclear or past review | [NZ ASA influencer guidance](https://asa.co.nz/codes/guidance-notes/guidance-note-on-influencer-advertising/). Affiliate commissions can make content advertising; identification must be clear. A human records their assessment and a private evidence reference. The configured review date is internal policy. |
| RETENTION-REVIEW | A conversion is past its chosen retention review date | [NZ Privacy Principle 9](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/9/). Retention depends on lawful purpose. There is no universal duration in this system. |
| PARTNER-RECORD | Active partner lacks owner, agreement reference or current review date | Internal review policy, not a legal requirement inferred from the sources. |
| OFFER-TERMS | Active offer lacks an owner or terms reference | Internal review policy. |
| DECISION-EVIDENCE | A non-pending decision has no matching recorded evidence | Internal review policy. The owner can alter the database; history is not tamper-proof. |

For Australian personal information, [OAIC APP 11 guidance](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines/chapter-11-app-11-security-of-personal-information) describes protection and destruction or de-identification when information is no longer needed, subject to exceptions. Confirm whether the business is an APP entity and which other retention duties apply. Our retention reminder does not decide applicability or perform disposal.

NZ, AU and other market labels identify the inspection. The general evidence gap check runs for all markets; it does not apply NZ advertising law to Australian placements. Add the correct market's rules and sources before making a compliance claim. Regulated financial, health, gambling and children's advertising need specialist review. The base makes no automatic legal classification.

Recorded evidence is a pointer, not the evidence file. Protect the original capture, contract or decision note separately. Review the purpose of any identifying references, restrict access, and include generated reports and backups in the retention plan. No deletion happens automatically. Refreshing a retention date records the operator's reason; it does not make retention lawful by itself.
